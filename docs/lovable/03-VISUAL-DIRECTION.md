## Current visual problem
The supplied mobile recording shows a polished but generic composition:
- black full-screen background
- gold eyebrow text
- giant white serif headline
- gold-highlighted word
- centered body copy
- stacked rectangular CTAs
- marble statue
- dark bordered feature card
- generic luxury authentication panel

These are useful ingredients, but the combination currently reads as a generic luxury/AI landing page.

## New visual thesis
ANCIENT FOUNDATION × FUTURE CIVILIZATION × LIVING BROTHERHOOD

Not:
BLACK WEBSITE × GOLD TEXT × STATUE

## Material language
Heritage: white marble, warm stone, carved surfaces, muted gold metal
Future: smoked glass, mineral black, cobalt digital light, subtle interface grids, translucent layers
Human: portraiture, movement, training, quiet candid moments, group/community presence

## Composition language
Prefer asymmetry, split-screen editorial layouts, full-bleed imagery, overlapping materials, vertical architectural rhythm, selective large typography, tiny metadata, strong separators, immersive transitions.

Avoid centered-everything layouts, four identical cards in every section, equal visual weight for every section, repeated bordered rectangles, and giant black voids with one sentence.

## Color roles
Base 1: mineral black
Base 2: charcoal
Base 3: warm ivory
Accent 1: cobalt / digital blue
Accent 2: muted gold
Support: stone grey

Blue should create the modern identity.
Gold should create the heritage and achievement identity.
