# ORANOS — LOVABLE MASTER BUILD PROMPT

You are building the new visual/product experience for ORANOS.

This is a serious premium product redesign and greenfield frontend build.

Do not make another generic AI-generated landing page.
Do not make a generic black-and-gold luxury website.
Do not make a startup SaaS template.
Do not make a portfolio-style page.
Do not make a collection of unrelated sections.

## 1. PRODUCT IDENTITY
ORANOS is a premium Greek-inspired fitness, lifestyle, mindset, and discipline ecosystem.

The four pillars are:
FITNESS
MINDSET
LIFESTYLE
DISCIPLINE

ORANOS is the parent ecosystem.
Inside the ecosystem are:
- OTERNAL — a separate AI Mentor product/application
- Titan Challenge — a structured 7-day proof-of-discipline experience
- Titan Pass — an exclusive membership identity manually issued by ORANOS
- Oranos Community — the human/community layer
- My ORANOS — authenticated member product
- Admin — internal operational product

OTERNAL must remain its own product experience. Never render it as a chat widget embedded inside the ORANOS homepage.
Titan Pass is NEVER auto-issued after passing Titan Challenge.

## 2. THE FEEL WE NEED
The design should feel like entering a future civilization / modern brotherhood with ancient foundations.

Blend these ideas carefully:
- Greek classical heritage
- contemporary Asian urban/digital atmosphere
- premium fashion/editorial art direction
- modern fitness culture
- digital community identity
- disciplined, mature masculinity without becoming aggressive or gym-bro
- technology without cliché sci-fi

The Asian influence must feel contemporary and architectural, not stereotypical.
Do not add random Asian characters, dragons, temples, anime visuals, or cultural clichés.

Think: ancient stone + modern glass + restrained technology + urban night atmosphere + human community + high-end editorial design.

The emotional target is:
this is a world I want to belong to.

Not:
this is a website explaining a fitness club.

## 3. VISUAL DIRECTION
Build a distinctive art direction instead of relying on default Tailwind/card aesthetics.

Core materials:
- dark mineral / volcanic black
- warm ivory stone
- subtle marble
- brushed metal
- translucent smoked glass
- muted cobalt/royal blue as the future-facing accent
- very restrained warm gold for heritage / achievement states

Gold must NOT dominate the experience.
Use blue strategically to establish the future/digital identity.
Gold is for heritage, Titan, achievement, and selected emphasis.

Avoid large areas of flat pure black with centered serif text.
Avoid default gradients, rainbow gradients, excessive glow, excessive glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, and floating SaaS dashboard patterns on the marketing homepage.

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY
Create a sophisticated typography system with contrast between:
- architectural display type
- editorial serif / human voice
- highly legible modern sans for product UI

Use typography as a structural tool, not decoration.
Do not use giant centered text for every section.
Create asymmetric compositions, editorial captions, section metadata, subtle numbering, strong whitespace, and cinematic visual hierarchy.

## 5. WORLD-BUILDING
The homepage must establish ORANOS as a world.
It should feel like a destination, not a brochure.

Possible visual language:
- architectural planes
- subtle horizon/grid structures
- sculptural figures
- macro material photography
- abstract topographic / contour lines
- controlled digital data motifs
- atmospheric city-light reflections
- premium monochrome human photography
- subtle community imagery

Technology motifs must be restrained and purposeful.
No circuit-board clichés.
No robot imagery.
No fake futuristic city wallpaper.
No random 3D objects just to fill empty space.

01 — OPENING / WORLD

06 — TITAN

09 — FINAL INVITATION
A strong, restrained invitation into the world.

## 15. INTERACTION DESIGN
Motion should support hierarchy.
Use slow controlled reveals, subtle opacity/scale changes, intelligent hover states, section transitions, and restrained scroll-linked movement.
Respect reduced-motion preferences.
Avoid aggressive parallax, particle spam, flashing effects, fake loading screens, and animation on every element.

## 16. RESPONSIVE DESIGN
Design mobile intentionally.
Test 360px, 390px, 412px, 768px, 1024px, and 1280px+.
No horizontal overflow.
No clipped typography.
No giant empty gaps.
No awkward desktop layouts squeezed onto mobile.
